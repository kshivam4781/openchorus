# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OpenChorus to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their age, body,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Credit other people's work, including the open models this project builds on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Using the project to generate content that infringes other artists' rights or
  impersonates real people.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/kshivam4781/openchorus/security/advisories/new)
or by opening an issue asking for a private contact. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, code or issues and may ban contributors who break
this code.
